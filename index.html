SAFETY FIRST

Clean smart. Stay safe.

Every cleanup starts with responsible preparation. Review these simple guidelines before joining a drive.

🧤 Wear protective gloves

Always wear suitable gloves while collecting waste. They help protect your hands from sharp or dirty objects.

⚠️ Never touch hazardous waste

Do not handle medical waste, chemicals, unknown substances or other hazardous materials. Inform the cleanup coordinator instead.

💧 Stay hydrated

Carry drinking water and take regular breaks, especially during outdoor cleanup activities.

👥 Follow the team leader

Stay with your assigned group and follow instructions from the cleanup coordinator throughout the event.
